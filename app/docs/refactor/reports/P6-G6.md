## Decisions of the group

Source: `migration/refactor/log.md` and the handoffs of the steps.

- W28 (beforePaint): QRCodeGenerate (`hooks/useGenerateQrCode.ts`) and ViewDocuments (`hooks/useViewDocuments.ts`) use the `beforePaint` option of `useFlowState`, because their old blocks ran OnReady before paint.
- ConnectShare, finding W23 withdrawn: the first review suspected a W23 deviation in the delayed upgrade popup. The tester compared the page with the old port at HEAD. After an unmount the popup is not started or is dropped, as in the old port. W23 holds.
- New domain `api/share` (W8): `Get_ShareDetails` for ConnectShare, and 3 calls for LegacyShareObject (with `keys.ts` and `hooks.ts`).
