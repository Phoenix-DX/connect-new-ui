  `HouseholdDetails_Block` does not count: P2 moves that block and its tree. Without this rule, Profile (G1, it renders
  the household preview) pulls 25 shared blocks (share, notes, contact) into G1. Each of these rows has a note.
- `target` keeps the folder of a shared block (`<folder>/<Name>/<Name>.tsx`), because the block folder also holds the
  test, the story, `mocks.ts`, `README.md` and `__captures__/`. A `ScreenBlock_*` file is one file
  (`pages/<R>/components/<Name>View.tsx`).
- 6 rows are merges: their target is a file that another row or another step also writes.
  - `Dialog_Navigation` goes into `NavigationDialog`.
  - `Dialog_AccessRestrictedAction_Legacy` goes into the platform dialog of P1-2.
  - The 4 `CacheControl_*` blocks become query hooks in `api/<domain>/hooks.ts`.
- No row has `review` in `notes` after the gate review (2026-10-22). 15 rows had it before; the gate review decided each one and the rows now carry the decision as a note.
- 7 rows moved to phase P5: `CacheControl`, the 4 `CacheControl_*` blocks, `CacheControl_UserInfo_Wrapper` (P5-4 rewrites them where they are; P6 only moves the file) and `Directory_Updates` (target `src/mocks/handlers/update.ts`).

## Candidate groups of `refactor-plan.md` §2.1 item 4

Each group gives the verdict (same, partly, different), the evidence and the plan. R3 applies to every merge: the
legacy class names stay, and a merged component renders the DOM classes of each variant it replaces.

### Group 1: email screens (7 screens, 2.6k lines)

Verdict: **partly**. Two families, plus a third form outside the list.

Evidence:

- The 5 `ContactByEmail*` screens (UBTConnect.ContactFlow) have one tree: `Layout_DetailPage` > `ContactByEmailForm`
  (shared block, "Add a subject" / "Add a message") > a contact card in the placeholder. No data action. They call
  `useNoCommonUi()`.
  - Person card (Member, OrbitHost): `CustomContainer` > `Columns_Three` > `UBT_LazyImage` (Base_52, class
    `ubt-object-fit-avatar-img`) + name + locality `IconText`. jscpd: 4 clones between the two `index.tsx` (53, 29, 20
    and 19 lines).
  - Organization header (OneSchoolGlobal, LDMCoordinator, OrbitHelpdesk): `div.text-align-center` > `CustomContainer`
    `contact-by-email__header` > `img.contact-by-email__header__img`. Only the image source is different. jscpd: 49,
    22 and 10 lines.
  - What differs: the title ("Contact Member", "Contact the X household", "Email"), `inIsPrimaryColor`, the first
    subject (`inSubject`) and `inUseDynamicTextAreaSubject`.
  - The send action differs too. Member and OneSchoolGlobal call `ServiceAPISendContactMemberEmail`. LDMCoordinator
    calls `ServiceAPISendContactLDMCoordinatorEmail`. OrbitHelpdesk and OrbitHost call
    `UBTConnect/ActionSendContactEmail`.
  - The flow after the send differs (audit events, message, back navigation).
- `EmailContact` (`Connect2_OrbitMobile_MUI.Orbit_ScreenBlocks.ScreenBlock_ContactForm`, 399 lines) and `EmailTarget`
  (`Connect2_LegacyShared.Email.ScreenBlock_Household_Email`, 329 lines) are a different family: `CommonUI_InsideLayout`,
  `Layout_Orbit` or the Connect2 `Layout`, the Connect2 `InputCounter`, `DEPRECATED_ButtonLoading`,
  `NavigationConfirmationDialog`, the platform `span.input-text > input` and `textarea`. The two are partly the same.
  They share the `Input_Subject` markup and `label.mandatory OSFillParent`. The header, the button classes
  (`btn btn-primary full-width` against `btn btn-primary OSFillParent`), the layout and the send action
  (`Connect2_OrbitMobile_MUI/ActionSendContactEmail` against `Connect2_LegacyShared/ActionUserContactUser`) are different.
  jscpd: `ContactByEmailLDMCoordinator/actions.ts` and `EmailTarget/components/actions.ts` share 16 lines
  (the application-event helpers).
- `LDM_Email_V2` (`ScreenBlock_Event_Email_V2`, 532 lines, G5) is a third form of the second family. The plan does not
  list it.

Plan:

- Keep the 7 routes and 7 pages.
- G2: `components/contact/ContactEmailForm` (from `ContactByEmailForm`, same DOM), `ContactPersonCard` and
  `ContactOrganizationHeader` (new, from the two card variants). Each of the 5 pages gives its config (title, colour,
  subject, text-area mode, card). Each page also has its own hook `use<Route>Send` that keeps its order of calls.
- The plan note of G2 says "one `ContactForm` container + config". The tree gets no fetched data (only route inputs).
  Only the submit differs, so a callback prop is enough and criterion 1 is not needed.
- Decided (Q6, option a, auto-accepted under the delegation of the user, 2026-10-22): no container.
- `EmailContact`, `EmailTarget`, `LDM_Email_V2`: keep their views. In G2 move the shared application-event helpers
  (16 lines) to `api/contact`. Do not merge the forms: the DOM differs.

### Group 2: dialogs (6 blocks)

Status: **done in P7-2** (see "Result of P7-2" below the plan).

Verdict: **partly**. Two base dialogs render the same DOM. The 6 blocks are wrappers or presets of them.

Evidence:

  `dx-dialog-header`, `dx-dialog-body`, `dx-dialog-footer` with `btn` and `btn btn-primary`):
  `shell/dialogs/ActionConfirmationDialog` (opened by `confirmAction` with a scope),
  `NavigationHandler/NavigationConfirmationDialog` (opened by a navigation event through `PreventBackNavigation`) and
  `shell/navigation/DialogExitConfirmation` (adds `dx-exit-confirmation-dialog`). jscpd: 12 lines (164 tokens) and
  12 lines (100 tokens).
- `Dialog_CustomChoice` (Menu, Profile): wrapper `connect-dialog-popup connect-dialog-popup--custom-choice`
  (`--vertical-buttons` when stacked) > `ActionConfirmationDialog`; header `CustomContainer` (margin bottom Base,
  Neutral10, SemiBold) > `text-align-center`; body `CustomContainer` (margin bottom Medium).
- `Dialog_Navigation` (7 routes): the same header and body containers, wrapper `connect-dialog-popup--navigation` >
  `NavigationConfirmationDialog`; labels as expressions (default "Yes" / "No").
- `Dialog_AccessRestrictedAction_Legacy` (12 routes): only an `OSBlock` element around
  `shell/dialogs/DialogAccessRestrictedAction`.
- `Dialog_GoExternalApp` (HomeFeed only, in `screens/HomeFeed/components/`): `ActionConfirmationDialog` with
  `subscription-pop-up connect-dialog-popup connect-dialog-popup--access-restricted`, an icon and a `CircleButton`.

  `upgrade-subscription-action-dialog backwards-compatibility`, confirm opens the subscription link.

- Presets, no merge: `Dialog_GoExternalApp` → `pages/HomeFeed/components`, `FreeSubscriptionActionDialog` →

  `platform/navigation` and uses the DOM of `Dialog` through `app/`.

Result of P7-2 (evidence: `src/components/ui/Dialog/Dialog.README.md`, `src/app/dialogs.ts`):

  `platform/navigation`.
- `Dialog_AccessRestrictedAction_Legacy` is its own module, `platform/feedback/dialogs/DialogAccessRestrictedActionLegacy.tsx`,
